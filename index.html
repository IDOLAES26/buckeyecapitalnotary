Common questions
Where are mobile services available?

Mobile appointments are available in and around Columbus, Ohio.

Where can we meet for a mobile notary appointment?

We meet at public, business, and facility locations in and around Columbus, Ohio. We do not meet at private residences.

  • Libraries
  • Starbucks
  • FedEx Office / Kinko’s
  • Post offices
  • Hospitals
  • Nursing homes
  • Funeral homes
  • Lawyers’ offices
  • Other suitable business locations

All locations are subject to operating hours, suitable space, access or permission where needed, and confirmation before your appointment. Choose a listed location using the meeting-location search in the form. If your facility or office is missing, contact us to arrange the appointment.

How much does an appointment cost?

Request a quote for your specific appointment. Notarization and any applicable travel charges will be explained before you confirm.

Can I schedule a virtual appointment now?

Virtual Notary is coming soon. You may prepare an inquiry for that service, but virtual appointments are not yet available.

Are estate planning and courier services available?

Both are coming soon. Their scope, availability, and booking details will be announced at launch.

Can a notary give me legal advice?

Notary services do not include legal advice. Direct questions about document selection, legal rights, or estate planning decisions to a licensed attorney.
